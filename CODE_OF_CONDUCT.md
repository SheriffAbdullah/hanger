# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Hanger to be a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their experience level, background, identity, or role. Developers and IT / tech-ops people are on the same side here: the hang is the shared problem.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it the same way.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and expertise, in whichever role they work.
- Never post real machine data in an issue: hostnames, usernames, serial numbers, internal software names. Use the synthetic sample or anonymized output.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory language.
- Treating a group, such as IT teams or developers, as an adversary, or mocking the people on the other side of a ticket.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge inappropriate. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub.
